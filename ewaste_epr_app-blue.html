Basis for each calculation

  1. Target % — set by the recycling-target schedule in the E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022: 60% for FY 2023-24/2024-25, 70% for FY 2025-26/2026-27, 80% from FY 2027-28 onward.
  2. EPR target (MT) — Annual E-Waste Generated × the Target % for the selected year.
  3. Registration fee — read off the CPCB fee slab against the computed target, not the raw generated quantity: under 50 MT ₹2,500; 50–100 MT ₹7,500; 100–1000 MT ₹1,50,000; 1000–5000 MT ₹10,00,000; above 5000 MT ₹15,00,000.
  4. Status — "Compliant" once actual recycled MT (self-reported) meets or exceeds the target; otherwise "Non-Compliant."
